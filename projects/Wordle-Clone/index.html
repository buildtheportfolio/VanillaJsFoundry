How to play

Guess the five-letter word in six tries. After each guess the tiles show how close you were.

  • W is in the word and in the right spot.
  • O is in the word but in a different spot.
  • R is not in the word.

Hard Mode forces you to reuse every hint you have found: green letters stay in place and yellow letters must appear in later guesses. Switch it on in Settings before your first guess.

Daily gives everyone the same word each local day. Practice is unlimited and does not affect your statistics.
